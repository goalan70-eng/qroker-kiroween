## 10. Why exactly 45 degrees at inset 2, and why 200 of 200

The focus vertex of the 2 x 2 board lies at raster position (172.598, 236.598) px, the image centre (the rotation centre) at (173, 237): the offset (-0.402, -0.402) px has equal components because both raster dimensions are rounded up by the same 0.8 px (345.2 to 346 px and 473.2 to 474 px) while the vertex is 43.15 and 59.15 modules, i.e. 172.598 and 236.598 px, from the origin. Rotated by exactly 45 deg, that offset becomes (0, -0.568) px, so the vertex, and with it two central markers, lie exactly on the pixel column x = 173.000, and the four central markers sit on the image axes at distance 1.414 x 6.5 x 4 = 36.77 px from the vertex. The pixel phase of every marker is therefore the same in all 200 trials of the cell (only the card patterns change). At inset 2 the BR marker has the phase (x 0.00, y 0.20 px) and its staircase outline has a blunt tip: the pixel-corner convex hull (866.0 px^2) is larger than the quadrilateral inscribed in its vertices (730.0 px^2), ratio 0.843. At inset 1 and 1.5 the same marker has a different y phase and ratios of 0.892 to 0.912. The 0% is thus the result of ONE pixel phase, not a failure rate.

The failure is not specific to the exact angle (section 3: 44.9, 44.99, 45.01 and 45.1 deg at inset 2 fail 10 of 10, 44.5 and 45.5 deg do not; at inset 1.5, 44.5 and 45.5 deg lose 1 of 10) and not specific to inset 2 (section 4: with the image shifted by a fraction of a pixel at exactly 45 deg, inset 1 loses 20 of 160 trials, inset 1.5 10 of 160 and inset 2 40 of 160, always by a silent gate drop). It is a sensitivity of the detector's marker-like gate to the pixel phase of a near-diagonal marker at 4 px per module.

## 11. Real detector sensitivity or harness artifact?

Both, with different roles. **Detector limitation (cause):** the gate value 0.85 has a thin margin. A valid marker at 4 px per module produces ratios between 0.843 (board image) and 0.912; isolated rendered markers reach 0.861 (section 6); at 3 px per module (edge 21 px) 3.9% of the isolated views and at 2 px per module (edge 14 px = `DEFAULT_MIN_SIZE_PX`) 25.8% are below 0.85 and are dropped silently. The ratio falls with marker size; the likely reason is that the pixel-corner hull of a diagonal staircase edge adds a fixed fraction of a pixel on every side, which weighs more on a smaller marker (not isolated here). **Harness property (amplifier):** rotating a 2 x 2 board about its own centre by exactly 45 deg makes the pixel phase of the four central markers identical for all trials, so a marginal ratio turns into a 0% or 100% result for the whole cell. Cells with camera rotation NONE and no tilt have no warp at all, so their pixel phase is also fixed for a given layout; RANDOM-rotation cells sample many phases. The stored 100% at 45 deg for inset 1 and 1.5 therefore do not show that those insets are robust at 45 deg (phase-shifted: 140/160 and 150/160 correct, inset 2: 120/160; coarse 0.25 px lattice, 10 seeds each, not a rate for random phases).

The failures of the stored RANDOM-rotation cells are a different mechanism (section 5 and 7): the missing marker there is a listed rejected candidate (NOT_QUADRILATERAL), the camera angles are mostly far from 45 deg, and a lower gate recovers none of them. Of 1100 sampled failing trials of all stored cells, 924 fail in group clustering, 172 in marker detection with a listed rejected candidate and only 4 by a silent gate drop (3 of them in the 45 deg inset 2 cell, 1 in a duplex cell); in the stored sample the gate issue is therefore almost entirely the 45 deg family.

## 12. Approved condition: centerOffsetModules never comes from card identities or the board truth

Confirmed, no violation. In `src/optical/testing/geometry-trial.ts`: `markerCenterOffsetModules(g)` (line 554) returns `g.insetModules + g.quietMarginModules + CORE_MOD / 2`, where `g = geometryOf(p)` is built from the cell parameters (`p.insetModules`, the physical layout) by `physicalGeometry(...)`. `decodeScenarioImage(image, p, g)` (line 558) passes exactly that number as `centerOffsetModules` (line 560) when the cell is `MATCHED`, and nothing else besides the image. `runTrial` calls it with `(image, p, g)` at line 619; the scenario `sc` (cards, positions, errors) is not an argument and is used only afterwards by `classify`. The offset takes the values 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.5 for inset 1 / 1.5 / 2 (pinned in `geometry-45deg.test.ts`). The `DEFAULT` decoder-layout diagnostic cells use `decodeGroup(image)` with no option at all.

## 13. Reproducible defect? Proposed correction (NOT applied)

A reproducible limitation of production code exists (`src/optical/detect.ts`, `analyzeComponent`, the marker-like gate): a valid marker whose staircase outline gives a hull/inscribed-quadrilateral area ratio below 0.85 is dropped without a rejected candidate. Smallest correction: lower the constant `0.85` of that one comparison. What-if with a traced copy (gate value 0.80 / 0.75 / 0.70, same images, nothing applied): every failing gate-drop trial in sections 2 to 4 is identified correctly (45 deg inset 2: 50/50, phase table: 160/160 at every inset, fine sweep: all recovered), the number of correct trials never decreased in the 750 + 270 + 480 analysed trials, no false accept appeared, and none of the other 1097 sampled failing trials became correct or a false accept (section 7; 3 of 1100 sampled failures recovered, all from the 45 deg cell). A value of 0.80 keeps a margin of 0.043 below the lowest ratio seen at 4 px per module (0.843) and covers isolated markers down to 3 px per module (lowest 0.830); covering 2 px per module (lowest 0.780) would need about 0.75. The gate protects against non-marker blobs, so the false-positive side of the change is NOT measured here.

Expected effect on the stored sweep, by construction: lowering the gate only admits components with ratio in [new value, 0.85) to the later checks, so every trial without such a component is bit-identical. Known: the stored DEG45 inset 2 cell (200 trials, 0 correct) would become about 200 correct (verified on 50 seeds), the two other DEG45 cells with r = 3.175 mm are unchanged at their phase (minimum ratio 0.892); the r = 0 and r = 6 mm DEG45 cells were not re-run here. Not known: how many other stored trials contain a component in that band (distractor cards on 3 x 3 boards, rounded-corner voids, partial occlusion); the sample says few. Pre-fix and post-fix data must not be mixed; any application needs a full re-run of all 824 cells as a separate data set, the detector tests (`detect.test.ts`, the m4-1 measurement) and a check of the Tier 4 false-accept counts.

A harness-side remedy (also NOT applied): add a seeded sub-pixel translation (uniform in [0, 1) px in both axes, from the existing camera stream) to the camera warp, so that DEG45 and other rotated cells sample the pixel phase instead of one phase. This changes every cell with an active warp and needs a re-run of those cells. It does not remove the detector sensitivity; it only makes the sweep report it as a rate.

Established (synthetic images, the sampled conditions only):

- The 45 deg / inset 2 recognition failure is a marker-detection failure of exactly one central marker (BR), by the silent marker-like gate drop, in 200 of 200 stored trials by construction and 50 of 50 re-run seeds; rotation estimation, fragment decoding, clustering geometry and image bounds are not involved.
- The loss depends on the pixel phase and the angle (44.9 to 45.1 deg at inset 2; fractional-pixel shifts at 45 deg at every inset), so it is not an inset-2-only or exact-45-only effect, and the stored 45 deg cells measure one pixel phase each.
- `centerOffsetModules` is derived only from the inset parameter of the cell.
- No false accept occurred in any of the re-run trials (production reader); this analysis changes nothing in the stored zero-false-accept results.

Not established:

- Real-camera behaviour of any kind (synthetic only), including whether real markers of 3 to 4 px per module fall into the affected ratio band at a rate that matters.
- The rate of the failure over random pixel phases (the phase lattice here is 0.25 px with 10 seeds per phase; section 6 uses 1 degree and 2 x 2 phases).
- The effect of a lower gate on false positives, on detection of non-marker blobs, on 3 x 3 boards, or on any cell other than the ones named above; this requires a separate full re-run.
- Whether other stored failures also depend on pixel phase (the RANDOM-rotation NOT_QUADRILATERAL failures were not analysed beyond their stage and reason).
- The silent gate drop in the duplex cell (1 of the 4 in the 1100-trial sample) was not examined further, and the recorded failing seeds beyond the first 3 per cell (up to 20 are stored) were not re-run.
