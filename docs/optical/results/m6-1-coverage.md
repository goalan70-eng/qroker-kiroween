The pattern behind the wrong identifications is visible in the list: a falsely accepted word becomes another card of the same suit at an allowed transform for that slot (for example S-9 read as S-4), which the arrangement and duplicate checks cannot reject. ## Not covered (limits)

- Real cameras, real prints, real duplex printing and show-through: not tested here (Milestone 8).
- Corruption other than inverted data modules (blur, perspective, lighting) is Milestone 7; this task covers the independent-bit-flip model and gross composition errors.
- Radius-1 group-level behaviour for 4 or more flips is not enumerated (fragment level is in m4-2 / m4-3).
- Camera rotations were applied to flat scenes only; the physical boards are tested at camera rotation 0 with the three physical views.
